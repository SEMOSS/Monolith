CAC required

This site only accepts sign in with a Common Access Card (CAC) or PIV card. Your browser did not present a card certificate, so you cannot continue.

  1. Insert your card in the reader and wait until it is recognized.
  2. Close any other windows of this browser that have this site open.
  3. Select Try again and, when the browser asks, choose the certificate for PIV Authentication (on a Mac it is named Certificate For PIV Authentication, and it carries your name and DoD ID number). Do not choose Card Authentication, which has no name and cannot sign you in.
Try again

If the browser does not ask for a certificate after you insert the card, close the browser completely and open it again. If the problem continues, contact your help desk.
